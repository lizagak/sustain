Hi there! I'm {{ site.author }}.

I am an artist, researcher, and designer based in Brooklyn, New York. Currently, I am a Postdoctoral Fellow of Co-design of Technology with Children at the Joan Ganz Cooney Center at Sesame Workshop. I recently completed my PhD in Human-Computer Interaction at the School of Information at UC Berkeley, where I was advised by Dr. Niloufar Salehi.

My research focuses on how young people engage with the technologies that shape their everyday lives -- especially around issues like online harm, privacy, interpersonal surveillance, and algorithmic injustice. I use participatory methods such as Youth Participatory Action Research (YPAR), speculative design, co-design, qualitative interviews, and design probes to support youth as co-researchers, storytellers, and action-takers.

I have participated and published in academic research communities across CHI, CSCW, and IDC.

My work has been generously supported by the National Science Foundation Graduate Research Fellowship, the Center for Technology, Society, and Policy, and the Algorithmic Fairness and Opacity Group. At Berkeley, I taught INFO 188: Behind the Data – Humans and Values with Prof. Deirdre Mulligan and INFO 203: Social Issues of Information with Dr. Morgan Ames.

I earned my BA from Washington University in St. Louis, where I studied Computer Science, Mathematics, and American Culture Studies. My CV is available upon request.
